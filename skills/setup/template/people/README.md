# People, who is expert on what

One page per team member, `people/<first-last>.md`. The point is routing: when a fact here needs a person to vouch for it, these pages say whose topic it is. The daily digest also checks the names it hears in a meeting against these pages.

Scope rules:

- **In:** handle, role, the topics the person is expert on, and what they own or are the go-to for. Facts only, each traceable to a source.
- **Out:** anything HR, such as pay, performance or personal matters.
- Each page's `owner:` is the person themselves.

## The team

One line per person: `- [Full Name](first-last.md): role, main topics`.
