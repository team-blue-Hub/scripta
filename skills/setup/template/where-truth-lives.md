# Where truth lives

> Every fact has one official home. This page maps each kind of fact to it, and nothing else. When two places disagree, the home wins and the other gets fixed.

| The fact | Its one official home |
|---|---|
| Why we decided something | [`decisions/`](decisions/README.md), **here** |
| Who is expert on what | [`people/`](people/README.md), **here** |
{{status_row}}
| How to change this handbook | [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md), **here** |
| What a page header looks like | [`page-format.md`](page-format.md), **here** |
| Conversations, drafts, brainstorms | Chat and notes, **not truth**: nothing is decided until it has a dated file in `decisions/` |

## The rule behind the table

The faster a fact changes, the further from this repo it lives. Decisions and how the team works are stored here. A number that changes every week stays in its own tool, and a row here points to it. When the team starts keeping a new kind of fact, add its row.
